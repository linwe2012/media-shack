Same visual quality, slower encode = smaller file. HEVC uses presets (Slow is the daily sweet spot; Slower is best overnight). AV1 uses speed numbers (P5 fast-balanced; P3–P4 max efficiency).
Fine-Tuning
HEVC Fine-Tuning (libx265):
• Animation (`-tune animation`): tunes deblocking and B-frames for flat cel colors and crisp line art.
• AQ 3 (`aq-mode=3`): shifts extra bits into dark shadows & smooth areas where banding shows up first.
• Film / Grain retains organic noise; Fast Playback eases decode load.
On by default (HEVC Main 10 & AV1)
10-bit Color Depth (default: ON):
• On: Encodes in 10-bit (yuv420p10le, 1024 shades/channel) with smoother gradients, similar or slightly smaller files, and ~15–25% slower encoding.
• Off: Encodes in 8-bit (yuv420p, 256 shades/channel) for faster encoding and broader legacy player compatibility.
